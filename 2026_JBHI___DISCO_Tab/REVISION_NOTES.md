  it from here. In the rebuttal, state you reviewed it and cite only if genuinely
  relevant — do not add a citation you haven't read.

  directly answers "is DCR meaningful?" and cheaply strengthens the privacy section.

  review of, say, 50 synthetic Heart Failure records) — it's the cheapest way to
  answer R2's "is this clinically valid?" concern and you already list its absence
  as a limitation.
- Keep the `\begin{comment}` blocks in `03`, `06` — they contain the *old*
  promotional wording. They are not compiled, but delete them before the final
  submission so a reviewer opening the source doesn't see the retracted claims.
